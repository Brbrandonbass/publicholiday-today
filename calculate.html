How this business days calculator works

Pick a start date, an end date, and your country, and this tool counts every working day in between — automatically excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and official public holidays for that country. It's built for payroll, project deadlines, SLA calculations, and shipping estimates where you need to know exactly how many working days fall between two dates.

Public holiday data is pulled live from the Nager.Date public holiday API, the same source used across publicholiday.today, so results reflect the official calendar for over 100 countries.
