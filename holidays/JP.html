Japan has one of the most holiday-dense calendars in the developed world, with sixteen national holidays ("shukujitsu") spread across the year — many tied to nature, gratitude, and reflection rather than religion, such as Greenery Day, Mountain Day, and Respect for the Aged Day. Japan also uniquely applies a "Citizens' Holiday" rule: if a single weekday falls between two public holidays, it automatically becomes a holiday too. Celebrates young people who reached the age of majority (18) during the past year, marked with ceremonies and formal dress.
February 11, 2026Wednesday

Commemorates the legendary founding of Japan under its first Emperor.
