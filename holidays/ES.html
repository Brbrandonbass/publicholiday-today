Public Holidays in Spain 2026

32 public and regional holidays observed across Spain in 2026.

Spain has one of Europe's most layered holiday systems: a set of national holidays applies everywhere, but each of the seventeen autonomous communities adds its own regional dates — from Catalonia's Diada to Galicia's Letras Galegas day — and individual municipalities can designate two additional local holidays each year. That means the real answer to "is today a holiday in Spain" often depends on exactly which region you're in. Below is the full 2026 calendar, covering national holidays and the major regional observances.
