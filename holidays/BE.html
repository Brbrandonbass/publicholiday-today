Public Holidays in Belgium 2026

14 public and regional holidays observed across Belgium in 2026.

Belgium keeps a straightforward calendar of ten public holidays that apply nationwide across all three regions and language communities — a rarity in a country otherwise known for regional complexity. The calendar leans heavily on Christian feast days (Ascension, Whit Monday, Assumption, All Saints') alongside the Belgian National Day on 21 July, marking the 1831 inauguration of the country's first king. What Each Holiday Means
January 1, 2026Thursday
Nieuwjaar
The first day of the Gregorian calendar year, marked with fireworks, family gatherings, and a fresh start after the festive season.
April 3, 2026Friday
Goede Vrijdag
A Christian observance marking the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, a bank holiday for many Belgian employers.
April 5, 2026Sunday
Pasen
The most important date in the Christian calendar, celebrating the resurrection of Jesus Christ.
April 6, 2026Monday
Paasmaandag
The day after Easter Sunday, extending the Easter weekend into a four-day break.
May 1, 2026Friday
Dag van de arbeid
Honours workers and the labour movement, held nationwide on 1 May.
May 14, 2026Thursday

A Christian feast held 40 days after Easter, commemorating the ascension of Jesus into heaven.
May 15, 2026Friday

A bridge day some Belgian employers grant to extend the Ascension long weekend.

Pinkstermaandag
Falls the day after Pentecost and is kept as an extra spring public holiday.

Marks the 1831 inauguration of Leopold I, Belgium's first king, celebrated with a military parade in Brussels.
