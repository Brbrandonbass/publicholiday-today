Public Holidays in South Korea 2026

18 public and regional holidays observed across South Korea in 2026.

South Korea's holiday calendar runs on a dual system, mixing the solar calendar for civic holidays like Constitution Day and Liberation Day with the lunar calendar for its two biggest family holidays, Seollal (Lunar New Year) and Chuseok — both observed as three-day breaks. When a lunar holiday would otherwise clash with another day off, South Korea uses a substitute holiday system to guarantee workers still get the extra day. Below is the complete 2026 calendar.
