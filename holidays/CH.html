Switzerland is a special case: only one day, August 1st (Swiss National Day), is a public holiday in every single canton. Everything else — Epiphany, Corpus Christi, Assumption, even Christmas-adjacent dates — is decided canton by canton, producing 26 slightly different regional calendars across the country. Some cantons observe unique local dates found nowhere else, like Geneva's Jeûne genevois or Glarus's Näfels procession. Below is a combined 2026 calendar showing both the one nationwide holiday and the major cantonal observances.

What Each Holiday Means
January 1, 2026Thursday
Neujahr
The first day of the Gregorian calendar year, marked with fireworks, family gatherings, and a fresh start after the festive season.
May 14, 2026Thursday
Auffahrt
A Christian feast held 40 days after Easter, one of Switzerland's few nationwide public holidays.
August 1, 2026Saturday
Bundesfeier
Celebrates the founding of the Swiss Confederation in 1291 with bonfires and fireworks across the country — Switzerland's only nationwide public holiday.
December 25, 2026Friday
Weihnachten
The Christian celebration of the birth of Jesus Christ, Switzerland's other nationwide public holiday.
