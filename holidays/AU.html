Australia's public holiday calendar is a patchwork of national dates — like Australia Day, ANZAC Day, and Christmas — layered with state and territory-specific holidays such as Labour Day, King's Birthday, and the Melbourne Cup, which are only observed in certain states. Because holidays are set by each state and territory government, the total number of public holidays a worker gets can differ significantly depending on where in Australia they live.
