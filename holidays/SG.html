Public Holidays in Singapore 2026

11 public and regional holidays observed across Singapore in 2026.

Singapore's eleven public holidays reflect its multicultural population, combining Christian, Chinese, Malay/Muslim, and Hindu festivals into a single shared calendar — Chinese New Year, Hari Raya Puasa, Deepavali, and Christmas Day all appear alongside secular dates like National Day and Labour Day. When a holiday falls on a Sunday, the next working day is usually observed as a holiday instead. Below is the full 2026 calendar of Singapore's public holidays.
