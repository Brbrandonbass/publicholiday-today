Public Holidays in Germany 2026

19 public and regional holidays observed across Germany in 2026.

Germany observes nine public holidays that apply nationwide, but its federal system means individual states (Länder) add extra religious and regional holidays — a legacy of the country's mixed Catholic and Protestant history. Bavaria, for instance, observes several Catholic feast days that aren't public holidays in Berlin or Hamburg. This makes Germany one of the more complex European countries to plan travel or business around. Below is the full 2026 calendar, including nationwide holidays and notable state-level observances.
