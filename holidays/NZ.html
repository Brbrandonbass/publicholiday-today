Public Holidays in New Zealand 2026

23 public and regional holidays observed across New Zealand in 2026.

New Zealand observes eleven nationwide public holidays, including the culturally significant Waitangi Day and Matariki — the Māori New Year, added as an official public holiday in 2022. On top of the national list, every region also celebrates its own "provincial anniversary day" commemorating its founding, meaning the actual number of public holidays a Kiwi gets depends on which province they live in. Below is the complete 2026 calendar, covering both national and regional anniversary holidays.

What Each Holiday Means

New Year's Day
The first day of the Gregorian calendar year, marked with fireworks and family gatherings.

An extra rest day tacked onto New Year's Day, giving New Zealanders two full days to celebrate.

Waitangi Day
Commemorates the 1840 signing of the Treaty of Waitangi between the British Crown and Māori chiefs.
April 3, 2026Friday
Good Friday
A Christian observance marking the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, a public holiday across New Zealand.
April 6, 2026Monday
Easter Monday
The day after Easter Sunday, extending the Easter weekend into a four-day break.
April 27, 2026Monday
Anzac Day
Honours members of the Australian and New Zealand Army Corps who served and died in war, marked with dawn services nationwide.
June 1, 2026Monday

An official celebration of the monarch's birthday, observed on the first Monday of June.

Matariki
Marks the Māori New Year, signalled by the rise of the Matariki star cluster in the winter sky — New Zealand's newest public holiday.
October 26, 2026Monday

Commemorates the 19th-century campaign for an eight-hour working day, held on the fourth Monday of October.

The Christian celebration of the birth of Jesus Christ, the biggest family holiday of the year.
